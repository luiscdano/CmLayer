News & Updates

Monthly tech brief

🧠 Generative AI

AI assistants move from chat tools to integrated development layers: IDE integrations, doc generation, AI code reviews.

Why it matters: AI becomes a workflow layer, not just a feature.

My take: AI as amplifier for learning, debugging, and design.

🔐 Cybersecurity

Zero-trust and identity-first security dominate 2026 planning.

Why it matters: Design for compromise, prioritize traceability and control.

My take: Validate auth, logging, and access early—even in learning projects.

💻 Software Development

Developer Experience (DX) becomes a key success metric.

Why it matters: Maintainability and clarity rival speed.

My take: Reinforces clean structure, docs, reproducible deployments.
